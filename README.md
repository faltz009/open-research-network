# Open Research Network

A collective mind for researchers that stores ideas as generalized geometric shapes — a shared map of human knowledge, alive with the people working on it, and a teacher for anyone who enters.

The network holds a collective understanding of both ideas and the people who work on them — every concept connected to related ones across disciplines, every researcher situated in the intellectual neighborhood their work defines. Knowledge accumulates as living structure, with each new contribution finding its place in relation to everything that already exists, and the frontier becoming visible and measurable as the network grows.

---

- A shared, navigable map of concepts where every idea has a position and connects to related ideas.
- Researchers are placed on the map based on what they work on, so you can see intellectual neighborhoods and who is near your work.
- You can ask the map about concepts and it teaches by guiding you through the shape of understanding.
- The map shows who is researching what, and makes the frontier of knowledge visible.
- Powered by a geometric computer ([Closure SDK](https://github.com/faltz009/Closure-SDK)) and a human verification layer.
- Built openly by the Open Research Institute as public knowledge infrastructure.
- Already in the shape of Internet 2.0 — the internet of agents and minds.

---

Knowledge has always compounded across generations through distribution, and the Open Research Network is the next layer of that compression: a shared geometric structure that makes the full depth of human knowledge navigable by anyone who steps into it. The goal is a planet of geniuses — a collective mind where understanding is the act of finding your place in the map, and every researcher's work contributes to a geometry that teaches everyone who follows.
