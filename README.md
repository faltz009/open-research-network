# Open Research Network

A modern LLM in a datacenter consumes between 10,000 and 20,000 watts to answer a simple question, while every other human — including Einstein and Von Neumann — runs on 20 watts and generates a precise model of the world at whatever resolution the question requires, from first principles, on the fly.

Agents hallucinate because they have no stable model of reality to check against, and as the internet of agents grows that problem compounds — without shared geometric representations of reality, agents can't be made efficient, can't communicate reliably, and can't be trusted to reason about the same world at the same time. A brain maintains two things simultaneously: a model of itself and a model of the world. We're building both, starting from the [published geometric theory](https://github.com/faltz009/whitepapers) and working toward a navigable shared world that agents and researchers can locate themselves in and reason from.

## 01 — Build The World

Bootstrap the geometric landscape: concepts, sources, arguments, and relationships as structured carriers on S³, so different vocabularies can point to the same underlying structure and proximity means something real — two concepts are near each other because their content is near each other, not because someone decided to tag them together.

**Live demos:**

- **[Thought Engine](https://faltz009.github.io/open-research-network/pages/draw.html)** — a compact rule unfolds into a visible shape, showing how continuous structure becomes discrete form
- **[Shape Field](https://faltz009.github.io/open-research-network/pages/field.html)** — a signal enters a field and reorganizes it, with static shapes and motion coming from the same underlying operation

## 02 — Teach Defender

Defender's archive, values, attention, and style become the identity layer that turns a geometric landscape into a perspective — a mind that reads new material, locates it in the existing structure, and answers from within the world rather than about it. Reading becomes imagination, questions become navigation, and corrections sharpen the model over time.

**Live demo:**

- **[Egregore Wars](https://faltz009.github.io/open-research-network/pages/defender-loop.html)** — a minimal social field where an agent learns which groups restore or drain signal, then acts on that; the first toy loop for an identity operating inside a world

## 03 — Make It His University

Researchers, collaborators, critics, and schools of thought become positions in the same landscape, so the map shows who works near whom, which debates are connected, and where different fields are touching the same underlying structure without knowing it — the Open Research Institute as public knowledge infrastructure where the frontier is visible because the whole territory is mapped.

---

![](./assets/banner-04-network.png)

## 04 — Build The Network

People bring their own identities into the map: researchers verify their work, locate themselves in the shared landscape, contribute sources, and build relationships through the human verification layer, until Defender's Brain becomes one public mind-world inside the larger Open Research Network — a proof of the architecture before opening it to everyone.
