Open Research Network

A collective mind for researchers — a world modeler that stores ideas as shapes, knows the people working on them, and teaches by letting the field take form.

  • A shared, navigable field of concepts where ideas unfold as geometric states.
  • Researchers are placed on the map based on what they work on, so you can see intellectual neighborhoods and who is near your work.
  • You can ask the map about concepts and it teaches by guiding you through the shape of understanding.
  • The map shows who is researching what, and makes the frontier of knowledge visible.
  • Powered by a geometric computer and a human verification layer.
  • Built openly by the Open Research Institute as public knowledge infrastructure.
  • Already in the shape of Internet 2.0 — the internet of agents and minds.
