Open Research Network

The Open Research Network is a public knowledge infrastructure built by the Open Research Institute — a collective of researchers building the geometric layer of the next internet.

The network holds a collective understanding of both ideas and the people who work on them, with every concept connected to related ones across disciplines and every researcher situated in the intellectual neighborhood their work defines. Knowledge accumulates as living structure, with each new contribution finding its place in relation to everything that already exists, and the frontier becoming visible and measurable as the network grows.

The goal is a planet of geniuses — a collective mind where understanding is the act of finding your place in the map, and every researcher's work contributes to a geometry that teaches everyone who follows.
