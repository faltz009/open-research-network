Egregore Wars

Defender moves through a social field and tries to keep three values alive: truth, good, and beauty. Each group changes one part of that state, and Defender learns what each group does by contact.

Over time the network should get healthier: trusted groups grow into larger resource poles, harmful groups stay smaller because Defender patrols and prunes them, and Normies remain scattered background noise.
