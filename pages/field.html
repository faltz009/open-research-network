Shape Field

This page shows a field taking shape from a compact signal: the signal describes how the form is generated, while the dots are samples of that rule. Moving the resolution sliders changes how much detail is drawn.

The first forms are simple closed structures, then the same mechanism is used for biological shapes: a cell, a neuron, and a folding sheet of tissue. Static signals unfold into 3D space; moving signals add a time axis, so one form becomes a coherent sequence of states.

The point is a shape language: describe the form once, then let any compatible system unfold it at the scale, detail, and motion it needs.
